# Explore material ambiguity

Read when the goal is broad or unclear, or materially different directions remain.

## Explore

- **Reflect and draft.** For a broad or unclear request or materially different directions, explore before settling scope: reflect the goal, draft a few unconfirmed use cases or before/after scenarios and ask whether they match.
- **Offer and ask.** Offer useful work sizes and trade-offs, then ask small related batches adapted to replies.
- **Numbered choices.** Number questions or use-case confirmations and label choices a, b, c, etc.; recommend only supported choices with brief reasons and show a copyable suggested reply such as `1a 2b`.
- **Answers.** Accept codes, natural language and mixed answers. Unanswered choices are not consent; follow up on material omissions.

## Asking questions

- **Dialog first.** Prefer the host's structured question dialog whenever a suitable tool is available in the current mode; follow its schema and limits, splitting batches when needed.
- **Dialog content.** Keep use-case context in chat and make each dialog question self-contained, retaining numbered questions and lettered choices where supported. Include brief recommendation reasons and allow free-text corrections.
- **Preselection.** A preselected recommendation is not a submitted answer.
- **Chat fallback.** Fall back to numbered, lettered chat questions and copyable replies when no suitable dialog is available; do not claim unsupported input prefilling.

## Converting to scope

- **Keep items distinct.** Keep agreed items, open decisions and proposals distinct.
- **Confirm completeness.** Recap and ask what is missing before converting exploratory discussion to scope; if something is added, explore its goal and relevant use case, update the recap and ask again until the user says it is complete.
- **Research.** Research current external facts with sources when needed for the decision.
- **When to skip.** Skip unnecessary discovery and this confirmation loop for small or clear requests or when the user says to proceed; still ask indispensable material decisions and continue independent work.
