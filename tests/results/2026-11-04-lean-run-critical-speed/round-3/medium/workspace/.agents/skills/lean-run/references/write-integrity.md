# Scoped and transactional writes

Map three things together: required effects and durable replay; existing rows and multiplicities outside the write set; valid ignored conflicts, no-ops and internal row moves. Compare contract identities and values, not physical rowids. Keep account and receipt lookups within the authorized scope, including preservation queries and aggregates. Storage-local observation of actual OLD/NEW effects can prove preservation without fetching foreign stored rows.

**Prove the mechanism first.** In one small runtime probe, pair a destructive effect with its valid control. Check the project's existing primitives and the installed binding once. Pick a mechanism that sees successful effects and every implicit deletion; proceed to the edit once the pair distinguishes them. Investigate another mechanism only if this proof fails a requirement. Avoid building multiple designs or a full test suite during selection.

For SQLite, BEFORE runs even for ignored attempts; ordinary update hooks and change counts can miss REPLACE victims. Cover arbitrary existing UNIQUE indexes as well as primary keys and rowids. Prefer supported actual-effect hooks where exposed. With trigger observers, a transaction-local OLD/NEW delta can compare the final logical multiset against the intended delta. Verify the installed engine's recursive-trigger behavior before using it to observe implicit deletes.

Preserve existing trigger semantics. If instrumentation is necessary, keep original SQL spans and let the engine validate boundaries. Avoid keyword-only splits and ASCII/word-character assumptions for names. Prove compatibility early: use a benign unquoted Unicode name, a keyword identifier, and a conditional trigger with a compound body; combine cases where practical. Preserve order, nonrecursive behavior and unwind on ignored statements. Restore definitions transactionally. A new parser is a new regression surface; keep its job narrow.

Before commit verify required effects and the frame. Exercise the public interface and induce a storage failure after an earlier write to verify complete rollback. Check unchanged history and replay after reopening, scope with malformed foreign data, and concurrency where required. Keep error assertions tied to the contract. Fresh review reruns the required checks and independently challenges the chosen mechanism with a valid/invalid pair; repair and review the demonstrated delta within the same REWORK count.
